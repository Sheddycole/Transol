About Transol

Smart mobility solutions for modern cities and communities

Our Story

Transol was founded in 2018 with a vision to transform public transportation through innovative technology and customer-focused service. What began as a small startup with just three buses has grown into a comprehensive mobility solution serving thousands of passengers daily across the city.

Our mission is to make public transportation reliable, accessible, and convenient for everyone. We believe that efficient mobility is essential for thriving communities and sustainable cities.

Our Commitment

At Transol, we're committed to providing exceptional service that meets the evolving needs of our passengers. We continuously invest in new technologies, sustainable practices, and staff training to ensure we deliver the best possible experience.

Our core values guide everything we do:

  • Reliability: We understand that people depend on us to get where they need to go, on time, every time.
  • Accessibility: We believe transportation should be accessible to everyone, regardless of physical ability or economic status.
  • Sustainability: We're committed to reducing our environmental impact through eco-friendly vehicles and practices.
  • Innovation: We continuously seek new ways to improve our services through technology and creative solutions.
  • Community: We actively engage with the communities we serve and contribute to their well-being.
