Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a transport card?

You can apply for a transport card online through our website, or visit any of our customer service centers with a valid ID and proof of address. For student cards, a valid student ID is also required.

How long does it take to receive my card?

Once your application is approved, you can expect to receive your card within 3-5 business days by mail. Express delivery options are also available for an additional fee.

Can I transfer my card to someone else?

Standard cards are transferable between users. However, Premium and Student cards are non-transferable and require ID verification when used.

What happens if I lose my card?

If you lose your card, you should report it immediately through our website or customer service. A replacement card can be issued for a small fee, and your balance will be transferred to the new card.

Can I upgrade my card type?

Yes, you can upgrade your card at any time. The remaining balance on your current card will be transferred to the new card, and you'll only pay the difference in price between the two card types.

Are there any discounts for seniors or children?

Yes, we offer special rates for seniors (65+) and children (under 12). Please visit our customer service centers with valid ID to apply for these special cards.
